# ADR 0007: Bound-Safe MOA, Clamp and TED Numerics

## Status
Accepted numerical planning contract (Refs #34), using the parent-integrated Amendment v9 as canonical authority. Reconciled 2026-10-04; acceptance does not establish implementation, parser/native proof or game authority.

## Context
Historical audits of the preserved baseline (`backup/pre-revert-2.13` @ `a4ab2a5fd189c9451304087641a584d90fc249eb`) identified numerical defects and optional reduction opportunities. These are planned repairs/contracts, not assertions that current code already satisfies them:
1. **Fixed Big-M in Barrel Selection**: `lpBuilder.ts` hardcoded $M = 1000$ for replacement barrels. When negative accuracy modifiers (suppressors $-3\%, -5\%$) or synthetic fixtures were evaluated, the constraint falsely bound inactive barrels ($x_b = 0$), causing false infeasibility.
2. **Zero-Intrinsic COI Platforms & Missing Fallbacks**: Receiver check `if (baseCOI > 0)` skipped modular barrel constraints entirely when the receiver had `baseCOI == 0`. Constant-only presolve also omitted intrinsic fallback valuations when positive-COI barrels failed.
3. **Multiplicity Underestimation**: Summing reachable templates once underestimated worst-case negative accuracy on multi-copy physical slots.
4. **Precision Loss from Truncation**: Coefficients and RHS values formatted with `.toFixed(6)` rounded fractional numbers, causing exact boundary solutions to be rejected as infeasible by HiGHS.
5. **Redundant Binary Variables in Ergonomics Clamps**: Unconditional 3-regime clamping emitted 3 binary variables and 8 Big-M rows for `capped_ergo`, and blindly duplicated them for `effective_ergo`, even though structural bounds and non-positive equipment modifiers provably restrict values to $[0, 1000]$.
6. **TED Secant Validity**: The weight penalty $q(W)=\max(0,100-300/W)$ changes regime at 3 kg. A secant crossing 3 kg can lie above the penalty, overstate it and invalidate the resulting objective upper bound. Floating endpoint/slope/intercept construction also needs a complete outward enclosure.
7. **Floor Display Downward Deficit**: Naive ceiling rounding on binary64 floats suffers from multiplication roundoff ($0.043000000000000003 \times 1000 = 43.0$), causing display floor to be strictly smaller than the witness value.

The [accepted canonical #34 resolution](https://github.com/AhaiMk01/tarkov-weapon-optimizer/issues/34#issuecomment-5970763741) controls every shortened formula in this record. Retain the [#41 provisional MOA fence](https://github.com/AhaiMk01/tarkov-weapon-optimizer/issues/41#issuecomment-5966142296), [#36 independent equivalence/proof gates](https://github.com/AhaiMk01/tarkov-weapon-optimizer/issues/36#issuecomment-5974832944), and merged ADR 0004 operation contract. The [post-acceptance #40 review](https://github.com/AhaiMk01/tarkov-weapon-optimizer/issues/40#issuecomment-5977261158) additionally makes the pinned native/glue rebuild, full-precision structured primals and parsed-model verification explicit cutover prerequisites. No listed numerical defect is retained merely because ordinary examples fail to expose it.

## Decision

The following numerical contracts are mandatory proof prerequisites; clamp/presolve performance specializations retain their separate optional adoption gates:

1. **MOA Formulation, Correct Big-M & Edge-Path Coverage**:
   - For replacement barrel $b$ with $\text{coi}_b > 0$, define $c_b = \text{coi}_b \cdot K$ ($K = 100 / 2.9089$).
   - Row coefficients are unscaled: $\frac{c_b}{100}$ and RHS $c_b - L$ (no double-K multiplication):
     $$\frac{c_b}{100} A(x) - M_b x_b \ge c_b - L - M_b$$
   - Non-binding when $x_b = 0$ with $M_b = \max(0, \text{MOA}_b^{\max} - L)$, where $\text{MOA}_b^{\max} = c_b (1 - A_{\min} / 100)$.
   - Compute $M_b$ and all vacuity/omission decisions with proven outward enclosures. A row whose upper MOA bound is proved at most L is vacuous; an uncertain comparison cannot remove a feasible build. Replacement rows remain active on zero-intrinsic-COI receivers, and constant intrinsic valuations are not silently dropped.
   - Intrinsic Fallback Rows use $c_{\text{base}}=\text{baseCOI}\cdot K$ and outward-proved $M_{\text{fb}}=\max(0,c_{\text{base}}(1-A_{\min}/100)-L)$:
     - When no positive replacement barrels exist: emit unconditional $\frac{c_{\text{base}}}{100} A(x) \ge c_{\text{base}} - L$.
     - When positive replacement barrels exist: emit $\frac{c_{\text{base}}}{100} A(x) + M_{\text{fb}} \sum_{b} x_b \ge c_{\text{base}} - L$, omitted if a positive barrel is required by the rooted conditional occupancy graph.
   - Nonpositive/missing COI barrels fall back to intrinsic $c_{\text{base}}$.
2. **Conservative Occurrence-Counted Negative-Accuracy Lower Bound ($A_{\min}$)**:
   - Sum worst-case negative accuracy modifiers across reachable **physical slot instances**:
     $$A_{\min} = \sum_{s \in \text{Slots}_{\text{reachable}}} \min\left(0, \min_{i \in \text{Allowed}(s)} \text{acc}_i\right)$$
   - Defined as a provably valid conservative lower bound on installed accuracy (and $\text{MOA}_b^{\max}$ as a conservative upper bound on dispersion), preserving multi-copy placement without claiming an attainable supremum.
3. **Constant-Only Modular Presolve with Graph-Based Impossibility**:
   - Impossibility Predicate: A weapon under constant accuracy is provably incapable of meeting $\text{maxMOA}$ if:
     $$(\forall b \in \text{Eligible Positive Barrels}: C_b > L) \land (\text{fallback is physically impossible} \lor C_{\text{fallback}} > L)$$
     where $\text{Eligible}$ means physically feasible complete alternatives under rooted conditional occupancy and locks.
   - Fallback is physically impossible iff every valid physical build rooted from the weapon is required by slot hierarchy and locks to select a positive-COI barrel.
   - Barrel elimination ($x_b = 0$) propagates conditional required child slot rules.
   - If general conflicts/conditional occupancy prevent proving completeness, withhold presolve impossibility and delegate to the solver. Under provisional MOA, proved impossibility is internal model infeasibility with public incomplete/none. Both completed Precise and Fast model-emptiness searches use `termination: 'optimal'`, with `Provisional MOA model infeasible` or `Fast provisional MOA model infeasible`. Neither is game-physical infeasibility; termination carries completion only.
4. **Complete Arithmetic-DAG Enclosures, Parsed Rows and Proof Withholding**:
   - **Target versus represented model**: Preserve fractional inputs as represented binary64 leaves without rounding weight/accuracy/parts; declared constants retain their adopted real semantics (including $K=1000000/29089$). Use `n.toString()` shortest-round-trip serialization **with verified native parser equivalence**, not a claim that formatting alone proves native coefficients.
   - **Normative complete-DAG algorithm**: Every construction node carries an outward interval enclosing its target real value. Leaves are exact represented inputs or outward declared-constant/input-uncertainty enclosures. Addition uses `[a.lo+b.lo,a.hi+b.hi]`; subtraction `[a.lo-b.hi,a.hi-b.lo]`; multiplication min/max of all four endpoint products; division uses reciprocal/multiplication only with a denominator enclosure excluding zero. Round every lower endpoint down and every upper endpoint up. Min/max/clamps use monotone interval extensions; bounds, comparisons, mapping, conversion and serialization preserve the same enclosures.
   - Include **all** upstream uncertainty products, summations, scales, coefficient/RHS construction, Big-M, presolve decisions, physical-to-auxiliary bindings, secant endpoint/width/numerator/slope/intercept and objective normalization operations. For represented v and target `[lo,hi]`, compute the error upward as `max(abs(v-lo), abs(v-hi))`. Short analytic estimates are permitted only with verified assumptions and conservativeness.
   - The accepted $\gamma_3=3u/(1-3u)$ shortcut for $c(1-A/100)$ applies with $A\le0$ and verified normal/no-overflow arithmetic. With operand uncertainties $\Delta_c,\Delta_A$:
     $$\Delta_{\text{MOAmax}}\le\gamma_3|c|(1+|A|/100)+\Delta_c(1+|A|/100)+\frac{|c|+\Delta_c}{100}\Delta_A.$$
     The last term retains the coupled uncertainty product. Bound $M=\max(0,\text{MOAmax}-L)$ using operand/enclosure magnitudes, not the cancelled nominal M; the general DAG remains normative when shortcut assumptions fail.
   - For each affine row, enclose original-to-represented deficit over verified variable domains: an upward baseline is $\Delta_{\text{rhs}}+\sum_i B_i\Delta_{a_i}$, $B_i=\max(|lower_i|,|upper_i|)$, plus full-DAG mapping/evaluation/input errors. Domain changes, auxiliary bindings and **both sides** of continuous equalities are included. A proved-zero coefficient-error term needs no arbitrary finite bound; every nonzero-error term requires a justified finite magnitude bound. Prove finite domains for `rec_axis`, `price_axis`, `ted_axis` and relevant auxiliaries; a free `price_axis` with inexact `-0.1` at the price endpoint cannot pass this gate.
   - Apply >= allowances downward, <= allowances upward and continuous equalities as two outward inequalities. Verify the **actual emitted and parsed allowance**, not just nominal pre-format numbers, dominates the computed deficit. Candidate MOA/ergo/recoil/price/weight allowances and native feasibility tolerances are not universal error theorems. Integer-price exactness requires proved operations/quantities/sums; fractional prices and weights stay in the full-DAG path.
   - **Planned native prerequisite**: Pin HiGHS/core and wrapper commits, matching ABI/glue, emsdk/toolchain/build settings and artifact digests in a scripted reproducible build. Export structured full-precision primal objective/columns and trusted status/dual/gap before native-handle destruction. Configure small/large matrix thresholds explicitly and verify parsed-model readback or equivalence on that pinned build, including nonzero coefficients that might otherwise be filtered. Any supported rescaling carries complete units/error provenance. Remove the unqualified stock fallback; rounded pretty-output primals are insufficient.
   - If any row/domain/decision/parser/representation/native prerequisite fails, **withhold proof without changing the original problem**. Keep earlier verified witnesses; feasible/feasible with incumbent, incomplete/none without, unknown upper bound null and no-incumbent lower bound null. Termination independently remains `optimal` for an already completed search, `limit` otherwise. Do not widen independent-validation tolerance or invent part/weight/price restrictions to make the gate pass.
   - Independent validation evaluates the original **unpadded** model under the adopted `VALIDATION_EPSILON = 1e-6`; it establishes witness feasibility only, not optimum, infeasibility, completeness or game authority. Numerical outer relaxation plus objective correction is necessary but not sufficient for the separate native/domain/equivalence certificate. Already completed qualified proofs and earlier verified incumbents are retained under ADR 0004.
5. **Exact Clamp Semantics and Optional Specializations**:
   - The Stage 2 correctness repair requires the exact two-stage clamp on occurrence-graph bounds retaining negative raw values and values above 1000; remove artificial `rawHi = Math.max(1000, ...)` provenance. The general proved three-regime clamp is an acceptable Stage 3 baseline.
   - Regime specialization and second-clamp elimination are **optional Stage 4 reductions**, not already implemented correctness repairs. They require mathematical equivalence and the applicable measured adoption gates. Specialize continuous `capped_ergo` ($t$) from proved unclipped bounds $[rawLo, rawHi]$ and `raw_ergo` ($r$):
     - Wholly below-zero ($rawHi \le 0$): $t = 0$ (**0 binary vars, 0 Big-M rows**).
     - Wholly above-cap ($rawLo \ge 1000$): $t = 1000$ (**0 binary vars, 0 Big-M rows**).
     - Identity ($rawLo \ge 0 \land rawHi \le 1000$): $t - r = 0$ (**0 binary vars, 0 Big-M rows**).
     - Upper Saturation Only ($0 \le rawLo \le 1000 < rawHi$): $t \le r$, $t \ge r - (rawHi - 1000)z$, $t \ge 1000 - (1000 - rawLo)(1 - z)$ with $z \in \{0, 1\}$ (**1 binary var, 3 linear rows**).
     - Lower Floor Only ($rawLo < 0 \le rawHi \le 1000$): $t \ge r$, $t \le r + (-rawLo)z$, $t \le rawHi(1 - z)$ with $z \in \{0, 1\}$ (**1 binary var, 3 linear rows**).
     - Full Disjunction ($rawLo < 0 \land rawHi > 1000$): Full 3-regime clamp with $M = \max(|rawLo|, |rawHi|, 1000) + 1000$ (**3 binary vars, 8 Big-M rows**).
   - Second Clamp Elimination: For $factor=\max(0,1+\text{equipErgoModifier})\in[0,1]$, `gear_ergo = factor * capped_ergo` lies in $[0,1000]$, so `effective_ergo - gear_ergo = 0` is exact. For factor>1, eliminate only when its proved reachable upper bound is at most 1000; otherwise retain the exact upper-saturation clamp. Boundary adjoining regimes remain legal.
6. **Complete Node Secant and Objective-Frame Bound Proof**:
   - Split a root weight range crossing 3 kg into the zero-penalty region and the region at/above 3 kg. Verified node intervals cover the original domain and are enforced in the LP; weight-row allowances must not extend beyond a secant's proved validity domain. At/below 3 kg use the zero penalty; a singleton uses a constant without division.
   - For non-singletons, enclose q endpoints, positive width, numerator, slope and intercept through the complete DAG. The exact real secant lies below q by concavity on that node. With slope/intercept errors $\Delta_m,\Delta_c$, an upward enclosure is:
     $$\Delta_{\text{secant}}=\Delta_m\max(|W_1|,|W_2|)+\Delta_c+\Delta_{\text{mapping/evaluation}}.$$
     Use a sufficient represented downward intercept shift, e.g. `nextDown(c_tilde - shift)` with `shift >= Delta_secant`, and verify the actual parsed displacement outward. A fixed `1e-9` is usable only when it dominates the computed node bound; otherwise compute enough shift or withhold proof. Narrow intervals require the same denominator and uncertainty checks, not a shortened endpoint-only estimate.
   - For a linear objective, accumulate upward:
     $$\Delta_{\text{obj}}=\sum_j\Delta_{c_j}B_j+\Delta_{\text{const}},\qquad B_j=\max(|lower_j|,|upper_j|).$$
     Propagate weighted/augmented-Tchebycheff coefficients, signed active deviations, ideal/nadir values, normalization floors, scales/constants and auxiliary bindings through the same full-DAG algorithm. Nonzero-error terms without finite proved domains withhold proof; proved-exact free terms contribute zero.
   - **Objective-unit conversion is mandatory**: A raw TED/secant error is not added directly to objective error. First propagate it through the actual scalarization coefficients and scaled axis mapping (weighted ergo uses e times TED; $fE=10\,TED$ changes the corresponding coefficient). Tchebycheff includes its deviation maximum and 0.01 augmentation in that propagation. An already conservative penalty shift may be counted again only conservatively in matching units. Preserve endpoint frames `10*fE`, `-100*fR`, `-0.1*fP`; gaps/tolerance/padding cannot silently switch units.
   - Correct **only a qualified native dual bound**, never an interrupted primal or status-text objective: $UB_{\text{valid}}=\operatorname{nextUp}(UB_{\text{native}}+\Delta_{\text{obj}})$ with the accepted native tolerance/error frame. Re-score each verified incumbent with the original nonlinear objective; presentation may retain nominal score, but the proof lower bound is a downward enclosure that cannot exceed its true model score.
   - Global upper bounds include the incumbent and corrected bounds from **live and tolerance-fathomed frontier nodes**, including retained `closedBound`. Dropping a tolerance-fathomed bound may understate the optimum by the pruning tolerance. Unknown/unbounded required regions stay unknown: public upper bound and gap are null, never zero. Maxima, pruning and gap upper-enclosure comparisons use directed proof arithmetic before testing tolerance.
   - Withheld required proof uses feasible/feasible with an earlier verified witness or incomplete/none without one; completed qualified proof survives late interruption. These numerical contracts are model-relative: active MOA remains `provisional_model`, Fast remains approximate and neither gains physical certification from numerical correction alone.
7. **Precision-Dependent Bounded Rounding & Outward UI Submission Contract**:
   - `outwardSafeCeil(hi, decimals)` evaluated on precision-dependent safe representation domain $\text{MAX\_SAFE\_WITNESS}(\text{decimals}) = (\text{Number.MAX\_SAFE\_INTEGER} - 10) / 10^{\text{decimals}}$ for $\text{decimals} \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$.
   - Strictly validates `decimals` as integer in $\{1, 2, 3, 4\}$; withholds display rounding and returns `null` on unsupported precision, overflow, or representability failure, preserving raw witness $hi$.
   - Enforces strict postcondition `parsed >= hi`.
   - UI submission contract: zero-aligned grid or outward ceiling snap ($origin + \lceil \frac{hi - origin}{\Delta} \rceil \Delta$) on offset grids, backed by pre-submission guard ($\text{submitted\_cap} \ge hi$).
   - Evaluated under the provisional software model without claiming game-physics authority.
